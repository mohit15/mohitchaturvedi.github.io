Leadership Profile

Building the capability
that didn't yet exist

I am a senior technology leader with 24 years of experience shaping national infrastructure, data strategy, and digital transformation across the UK and India. My career has a single defining thread: I identify the data and analytical capability an organisation needs but doesn't yet have — make the case for it, build it from the ground up, and use it to fundamentally change how decisions get made.

At Smart DCC, I serve as the CTO team's trusted authority on data architecture — representing DCC at national regulatory forums (TABASC, DESNZ, CFDA), delivering £4M+ in cost optimisation, and rated top performer in two consecutive cycles. At Telefonica UK, I built the UK's first Smart Meter Data Lake from zero — and used it to surface a systemic fault affecting 700,000 devices that was completely invisible before the platform existed.

I am a strategic operator — equally at home advising at board level, presenting to regulators, and leading the teams who deliver the outcomes. I bring both executive presence and engineering depth, and I use both to drive real, measurable impact.
